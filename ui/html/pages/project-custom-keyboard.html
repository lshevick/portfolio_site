Custom Keyboard

A Dasbob keyboard designed, built and wired by hand.

Building my own keyboard was one of those projects that starts as a hobby and ends up teaching you more about electronics, firmware and patience than you'd expect.

I picked the case, laid out the keycaps, soldered the PCB and got it typing before I knew how to spell "debounce."

What's involved

  • Case & layoutChoosing a case and planning the key matrix.
  • KeycapsSourcing and arranging the switches and keys.
  • PCB & solderingWiring the rows and columns together.
  • FirmwareMaking it actually type on first try.
